anqa

Long-necked bird you almost never see. The mark is the approved Pakistani truck-art painting: black probe-neck, cyan eye, lilac and mango wings, floral inlay. Use that file. Do not substitute a geometric redraw. Typeset the name in product chrome with Fira Sans ExtraBold.

1. Primary lockups

Use the painted stacked lockup when there is room (README header, poster, merch). Use the clean stacked or horizontal lockup (bird + typeset word) in dense chrome (HUD about, site nav). Each lockup ships regular (ink type) and -on-dark (cream type).

9. Backgrounds

Preferred: Cream, Paper white, Ink. Colour mark may sit on Cyan or Mango only if contrast on the black neck still holds — never on Lilac (the tail vanishes). Photography: only quiet, out-of-focus fields; keep full clear space.

10. Incorrect use

  • Do not set the word in any face except Fira Sans ExtraBold 800.
  • Do not uppercase the wordmark. It is anqa, never ANQA in the lockup.
  • Do not stretch, outline, or add a drop shadow.
  • Do not recolour the eye. Cyan only (or knockout in mono).
  • Do not rotate the bird or flip it to face left.
  • Do not place the painted word on the horizontal lockup or below 80 px stacked.
  • Do not replace the bird with a new drawing. Recrop source/approved.jpg.
  • Do not put the four-colour mark on a busy truck-art field — the mark already is the art.
